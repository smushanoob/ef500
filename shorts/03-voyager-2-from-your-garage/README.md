# How to launch Voyager 2 from your garage (satirical 3D animation Short, 60s)

**Final video:** `voyager-2-from-your-garage.mp4` (1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, -14 LUFS)

A guy unboxes "VOYAGER 2 - SOME ASSEMBLY REQUIRED", the Titan IIIE-Centaur juts out of his garage roof,
glowing plutonium RTGs sit next to the garden gnome, the 69 KB flight computer meets a 3 MB selfie, the golden
record goes on, the planets line up, he rolls it to the "CAPE CANAVERAL (BACKYARD DIVISION)" lawn, launches,
stages over Earth, flies past Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune, and finally tells the neighbor it was a weather balloon.

## Facts used (all real)
Titan IIIE-Centaur ~160 ft; three plutonium-238 RTGs; ~69 KB total memory; golden record with whale songs and Chuck Berry;
the alignment occurs about every 175 years; launched Aug 20, 1977 from Cape Canaveral; Jupiter 1979, Saturn 1981,
Uranus 1986, Neptune 1989 (Voyager 2 is the only spacecraft to visit Uranus and Neptune); Triton's geysers; 13+ billion miles away.
