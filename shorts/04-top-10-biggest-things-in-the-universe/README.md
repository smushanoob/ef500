# Top 10 biggest things in the universe (true-to-scale 3D Short, 52s)

**Final video:** `top-10-biggest-things-in-the-universe.mp4` (1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, -14 LUFS)

Built from the channel analytics: ranked-list format, hook inside the first 1.5 s (no intro card), and an explicit
comment prompt at the end ("Which one broke your brain? Tell me in the comments" + on-screen COMMENT BELOW).

## Countdown (all sizes true to scale, computed in real kilometres)
| # | Object | Size used |
|---|---|---|
| hook | Earth | 12,742 km |
| 10 | Jupiter | 139,820 km (11 Earths) |
| 9 | The Sun | 1.39 million km (109 Earths, >99% of the Solar System's mass) |
| 8 | Rigel (blue supergiant) | ~79 solar radii |
| 7 | Betelgeuse (red supergiant) | ~700 solar radii (shown swallowing Mars's orbit) |
| 6 | Stephenson 2-18 | ~2,150 solar radii, estimate (shown past Jupiter's orbit) |
| 5 | Solar System to Neptune | ~60 AU, about 8 light-hours across |
| 4 | Orion Nebula | ~24 light-years |
| 3 | Milky Way | ~100,000 light-years; "you are here" ~26,000 ly from the core |
| 2 | Laniakea Supercluster | ~520 million light-years, ~100,000 galaxies |
| 1 | Observable universe | ~93 billion light-years, centred on us |

UY Scuti was deliberately not used: Gaia-era measurements revised it down to roughly 900 solar radii.
